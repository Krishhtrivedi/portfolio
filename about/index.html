I am Krish Trivedi, an independent AI developer and systems engineer based in Ahmedabad, India. Over the past several years, I have worked across the entire engineering continuum — from training predictive machine learning models and orchestrating multi-agent LLM systems to configuring perimeter enterprise firewalls and managing cloud server clusters.
